      Code systems

      NIC vs NAICS

      • NIC — National Industrial Classification (India). Used for company registration, MSME, and Indian statistical reporting.
      • NAICS — North American Industry Classification System. Common in US/Canada market and compliance contexts.
      • With NIC + NAICS selected, every top-5 set includes at least 2 NIC and 2 NAICS codes (when available), then ranked by similarity.

      Writing tips

      Better descriptions

      • Be concrete — name products or services, not vague goals.
      • Name customers — households, hospitals, factories, exporters.
      • Say the process — manufacturing, retail, software, logistics, farming.
      • Add scale — local shop, online marketplace, nationwide wholesaler.

      Good “We manufacture lithium-ion battery packs for electric scooters and supply dealers across Maharashtra.”

      Weak “We do business in the electric vehicle space.”

      Reading results

      Confidence & ranking

      • High confidence — description clearly matches one activity.
      • Low confidence — add products, customers, or process detail and try again.
      • Source tag — each suggestion shows (NIC) or (NAICS).
      • Related codes — nearby activities in embedding space; useful for edge cases.

      03 — Project

      About

      A semantic layer over curated industrial activity codes.

      Industrial Code Studio uses a fine-tuned multilingual transformer to turn free-form business descriptions into NIC and NAICS codes.

      Text is optionally translated, embedded, and ranked by cosine similarity. A short rationale explains each decision.
